# 周度综合总结

创建本周工作与思考的全面综合总结。

## 使用方式

```
/weekly-synthesis
```

## 分析流程

1. **汇总本周工作**  
   - 本周创建的所有笔记；  
   - 本周修改的所有笔记；  
   - 本周产生活动的项目。

2. **识别模式**  
   - 反复出现的主题；  
   - 共同面临的挑战；  
   - 关键突破时刻；  
   - 能量波动规律（哪些事让您充满干劲，哪些事令您精疲力竭）。

3. **提炼认知**  
   - 涌现出的关键洞见；  
   - 思维演进路径；  
   - 新发现的关联；  
   - 已解答与新提出的疑问。

4. **评估进展**  
   - 已推进的项目；  
   - 已维持现状的领域；  
   - 新增的资源；  
   - 已归档的事项。

## 输出格式

生成一份周度综合总结笔记：

```markdown

## Week at a Glance

- Notes created: [X]
- Projects active: [List]
- Major accomplishments: [List]

## Key Themes

### Theme 1: [Name]

- Where it appeared: [contexts]
- Why it matters: [significance]
- Next actions: [what to do]

### Theme 2: [Name]

- Where it appeared: [contexts]
- Why it matters: [significance]
- Next actions: [what to do]

## Major Insights

1. [Insight with context]
2. [Insight with context]

## Progress by Project

### [Project Name]

- What advanced:
- What's blocked:
- Next week's focus:

## Questions Emerged

- [Question 1 - and why it matters]
- [Question 2 - and why it matters]

## Energy Audit

- What gave energy:
- What drained energy:
- What to adjust:

## Connections Made

- [Note A] <-> [Note B]: [Why significant]
- [Concept X] <-> [Concept Y]: [New understanding]

## Next Week's Intentions

1. [Primary focus]
2. [Secondary focus]
3. [Thing to explore]

## To Process

- Inbox items: [count]
- Orphaned notes: [list]
- Missing connections: [identified]
```
